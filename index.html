✦ Our Story

ABOUT GARGO HAVEN

A 2+ of excellence in Mombasa's container logistics.

Mombasa's Most Reliable Container Depot

Gargo Haven Ltd was established in 2024 in Mombasa, Kenya, with a clear purpose: to provide the port's shipping lines, freight forwarders, and importers/exporters and all others with a professional, technology-enabled empty container depot and haulage service that meets international standards.

What began as a single-yard operation in Changamwe has grown into a network of depot partnerships across Mombasa's key container corridors — from the KPA main port and APM Terminals to Consolebase, Hakika, Kibarani, and Fortune Container Depot.

We are IICL-certified for container inspection and repair, KPA-licensed as an approved depot operator, and KRA-compliant with full customs documentation services. Our proprietary tracking platform gives clients real-time visibility of both containers and the trucks carrying them.

Mission, Vision & Contact
